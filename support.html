Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Open HOW TO PLAY from the title screen at any time. In short: block (受) as the third, loud clack sounds to parry, cut (斬) as your opponent recovers, and fill their posture to break their guard for a finishing blow.
Can I use buttons instead of the joystick?
Yes. Open SETTINGS and set CONTROLS to BUTTONS for separate block and attack buttons.
I keep losing to one witness.
Each opponent teaches something different. Read the "How they fight" note on their sheet before the duel, and watch for feints: trust the clacks, not the blade. After a loss, RETRY starts again right away.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Ink & Legend plays fully offline.
Is there sound and haptics?
Yes. Sound can be switched off in SETTINGS; haptics follow your device's system settings.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

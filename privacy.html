Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

This policy describes how Ink & Legend (the "app"), made by Suwitcha Sugthana ("we"), handles your information. In short: we do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use the camera, the microphone, your photos, your contacts or your location, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example, which opponents you have beaten, the clues you have heard, and your control and sound choices) are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The app is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
